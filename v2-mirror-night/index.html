    Automated Rx lens customization center.

    Every prescription runs through a digitally controlled line, from lens design to the final fit in the frame.

    • Sub-micron surfacing
    • End-to-end digital control
    • High-vacuum coating
    • Freeform custom edging

    1. Digital prescription design

      Each Rx becomes a freeform surface calculated for the frame's wrap.

    2. Sub-micron precision surfacing

      CNC generators cut the surface into the blank.

    3. Automated digital polishing

      Polishing follows the same digital file.

    4. Coating & colour configuration

      Tint, mirror and functional layers specified.

    5. High-vacuum coating

      Layers deposited in a vacuum chamber.

    6. Freeform custom edging

      Edged to the exact shape of each frame.

    7. Contour forming & verification

      Fitted to the frame and checked against the Rx.
