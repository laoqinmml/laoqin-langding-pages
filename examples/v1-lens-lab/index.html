More than a prescription dropped into a sports frame.

A good Rx goggle is designed around three things at once: the frame, the prescription and the sport. We check curvature, lens shape, Rx data, activity intensity and light conditions early, so you go through fewer sample rounds and carry less risk.

  • Clarity

    Rx compatibility checked against SPH, CYL, axis, PD, frame size and curvature.

  • Design

    Freeform surfacing keeps the wraparound profile intact.

  • Function

    Photochromic, high-contrast, polarized, anti-fog and mirror options.

  • Stability

    Field of view and fit balanced for movement.

  • Product

    A differentiated collection, not a catalog copy.

  • Production

    Sampling lowers development and delivery risk.
